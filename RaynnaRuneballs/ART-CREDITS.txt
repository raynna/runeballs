Faction frame illustrations and cartoon sprite atlas generated with the built-in
image_gen tool, November 2026, based on the user's visual direction. Source PNGs
and prompts are retained in ArtSource in the development workspace.
Runtime atlas conversion: tools/build_runeballs_faction_art.py.
Road segment and aiming dot are original procedural artwork.
No Zuma or Luxor artwork is redistributed. Warcraft faction imagery belongs to
its respective rights holders. This fan addon is not an official Blizzard product.

Native WoW UI textures and sounds are referenced from the installed client;
no extracted client audio files are bundled. Spell sound FileDataIDs are
from wowdev's verified listfile release 202611122246:
https://github.com/wowdev/wow-listfile/releases/tag/202611122246
Shoot: SOUNDKIT IG_MAINMENU_OPTION_CHECKBOX_ON (856), quiet UI tick.
Hit: 568461 sound/spells/fireballimpacta.ogg
Match: 568678 sound/spells/arcaneexplosion.ogg
Bomb: 568528 sound/spells/smallexplosions_generic_01.ogg
Frost: 569378 sound/spells/frostnova.ogg
Rewind: 569735 sound/spells/teleport.ogg
Combo, swap, win and lose use native SOUNDKIT entries, with client UI fallbacks.

Bundled Lilita One and Baloo 2 fonts are from google/fonts, licensed under SIL
Open Font License 1.1. Both license notices are in Media/Fonts. RuneballsLabels.ttf
is a renamed static weight-800 instance of Baloo 2. The variable source is retained
in ArtSource/Fonts. Font sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lilitaone
and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/baloo2
